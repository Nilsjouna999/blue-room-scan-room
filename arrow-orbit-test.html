Acceptance gate — in order, your eye is the instrument

  1. JUDDER (decides everything). Motion A, loop on. Sweep 277 → 439 → 574, once fixating a point on the rim, once tracking the arrow. If 439 shows a picket fence / multiple arrows while 277 is clean, the residual lag is sampling, not easing. Confirm by flipping smear 26° ON at 439 — if the fence disappears, it is proven and the smear is mandatory.
  2. LAUNCH. B vs A at 1× then 0.25×. PASS = at least 3 visible frames of growing gap, and the first visible position within one arrow-width of 12 o'clock. FAIL → raise the launch from 140ms.
  3. HAMMER. C vs A. PASS = an arrival, not a glide, with no teleport — at 0.25× you can count at least 2 decreasing steps. Teleports → lengthen the hammer; glides → shorten it. Never both.
  4. LOAD. D + smear 26 + tuned reverb. Sustained hog: should be indistinguishable. Launch spike: must still depart from rest. Seat spike: the orb dip must land on the stop frame.
  5. REVERB. Tuned vs C3-ring, watching the top rim. Any rise above the rest line = C3 fails (expected).
  6. SEAMS. D at 0.25×, watch the launch→cruise and cruise→hammer joins. Any kink is a slope mismatch in the numbers, not a perf problem.
