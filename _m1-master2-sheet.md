## 3. THE MASTER DESIGN SHEET

### NAME
**THE INHABITED ORIGINAL** — the shipped desk, re-tracked and repaired, with the archive alive **inside** its two sections, and the goods quoted verbatim.

### THESIS
The builder's own resolution, taken literally (`_m1-builder-signal.md`, ★★★, 2026-08-14): the original's structure — premium, contained, the lit object unmistakably the product — with the field's one proven mechanic (marks that answer attention) contained inside each reading's own section, never on the ground. The ground carries no information and no light. Everything that rewards attention lives where attention already is. The specimens are real engine output, so the desk sells with the goods instead of with plausible prose.

### GATE DESCRIPTION (no banned words)
A dark, silent ground. One lit parchment object stands in the middle distance, unmistakably the thing for sale. Near it — close enough to read against each other in one glance — the two ways in, each a dense little place of its own: each carries its name, one true sentence of what it is, a quoted line of what it actually says, and a small shoal of the real glyphs it is read from, which stir toward gold when that way in is looked at. Under the object, five lines of reading. Beneath everything, one quiet typographic line of words that are doors. Nothing is in a box; nothing arrives; the page is set at first paint.

### THE ARRANGEMENT
- **Wide (≥1200px):** the shipped three-region grid with the critique's one structural repair — `grid-template-columns: minmax(300px, 1fr) auto 330px` (item 7: the object into an `auto` track, residual width to ctrl; raise `.menu__draw-doors` max-width from 360px, one eyeball pass). The 330px region keeps the wall text; its two void cells are reclaimed by the exits row.
- **Base (unconditional, no media query):** stacked, in order: masthead → tarot section → object → berth → birth section → exits row. - **The section fields:** each door-section contains its own glyph shoal, absolutely positioned in percentages inside the section's box, `inset:12px`, `pointer-events` per the door rules below. With 28 marks total this is decoration-that-is-true, not a second Codex.
