# U1 — THE AUDIT OF THE CONCEPT SHEET

*The builder's own GPT audit of `docs/U1_CONCEPT_V1.md`, handed over 2026-08-16, the same
day that sheet was written. Recorded here because its central finding invalidates the
sheet's own title, and because a document that says "settled" outranks a chat message the
next time somebody reads this repo cold.*

---

## THE VERDICT

> Intelligent, unusually rigorous — **but it is not "the concept, settled."** It
> successfully explains a scroll-driven reading performance. It does not stabilize the
> interactive product showcase that was already being defined.

## WHY THE PROCESS KEEPS FLOPPING — three questions never resolved

1. **What is the hero?** The existing Blue Room card, or a new plate-and-paper machine?
2. **What is the showcase proving?** That the reading is derived, that the product is
   beautiful, or that the final record is worth keeping?
3. **Is this an addition to the locked main menu, or a new experience with its own
   material universe?**

★ **THE DECISIVE CONTINUITY PROBLEM.** The agreed direction had three constraints: the
main menu is **locked** and the showcase is an **addition**; the **real card** with its
controls, effects and system stays intact; the card sits **farther inside the Blue Room
surface, behind flush glass**, so you are looking into a showcase.

The sheet replaced all of it with a dark machined plate, cut slots, a separate paper
sheet, a wet meniscus, detached illuminated tiles, a fold, a wax seal, and a newly
composed final record.

> Each pass responds to criticism by **inventing another object language rather than
> refining the agreed container.** That is why it feels like starting over every time.

---

## WHAT THE SHEET GENUINELY SOLVES — keep these

- **The product argument.** `input → derivation → interpretation → kept record`. The
  showcase cannot merely animate decorative marks; it has to show the interpretation came
  from something.
- **The determination/interpretation split.** "Left of the seam is what the calendar and
  the name hand over; right of the seam is what the house makes of it." Wording needs
  work, the distinction is foundational.
- **Ending as delivery.** "Ending on the letter shows the house thinking; ending on a
  filed object shows it delivering." Sharp and product-aware.
- **Reversibility.** Position-based motion, no typing, no ambient loop, a true still
  point. **The 6% motionless hold especially** — stillness is not dead time, it is where
  comprehension happens.
- **The data-integrity finding.** That the six values are hardcoded and have already
  drifted from the engine is worth more than much of the visual specification. It should
  become an **implementation invariant**, not a warning inside a concept document.
- **The closing test.** One still frame first. Correct — though it is strange at the foot
  of a document titled *settled*: that test is the **gate**, not a polish check.

## WHERE THE DESIGN BREAKS

**Too many physical metaphors.** Archive, house, machined plate, cut slots, inked paper,
letter, wet edge, detachable tiles, folded record, wax seal, card. Each is individually
evocative; together they are material overstatement. Elegant product theatre establishes
**one physical law** and compounds inside it. Here every beat introduces a new one, so the
viewer admires the transitions without understanding what is physically happening.

**The transformation is not materially credible.** A rigid, machined, slotted plate cannot
participate in a paper fold without changing its material identity. Beat 4 half-dodges
this by detaching the lips as tiles, but still says *all of it travels*. The continuity
the sheet insists on is exactly what its motion cannot prove.

**The wet edge is clever but ungrounded.** A meniscus belongs to liquid or wet ink. It
suits paper; it does not explain digits rising through machined apertures. The shared
clock should be something **both** surfaces can plausibly receive — a raking band of
light, a scanning plane beneath the glass, a shared shadow edge, a moving line of focus
inside the chamber. A fixed keylight can stay fixed while an internal reading plane moves.

**Perfect simultaneity works against comprehension.** Landing both at the same instant
says "translation" conceptually but asks the eye to watch two loci at once. The better
micro-rhythm is **causal**: derivation reaches the lip → a shared edge crosses the seam →
the sentence settles → both rest. The delay can be tiny; it only has to make the eye feel
*because* rather than *alongside*. It also reverses better.

**"Checkable" is overstated.** Only life path is arithmetic. Sun sign and year animal are
**rule-based lookups** with edge cases and conventions; the seeded draws are deterministic
only if seed and selection method are visible. The honest categories are *calculated or
looked up from the supplied date* and *drawn deterministically from the supplied seed*.
The sheet also contradicts itself — it names Beat 1 "The Given", calls the left side what
the calendar "hands over", then says the surface never claims the marks are given rather
than chosen. **One consistent epistemic position is needed.**

**"Not correspondence" is still correspondence.** It rejects the psychic-letter costume
but keeps calling the paper *the letter*, gives it passages, and folds it like one —
relying on the cultural meaning of letter-folding. Removing salutation and signature does
not remove that reading. Either embrace a restrained letter, or **rename and redesign it**:
reading sheet, folio, archive leaf, interpretation page.

**The scroll contract is incomplete.** Six screen-heights are budgeted precisely, but not
how a reader experiences them while flicking, pausing, reversing across a boundary, using
keyboard or touch, requesting reduced motion, or resizing mid-scene. If rest is
load-bearing, **the interaction has to create rest** — broad positional plateaus or soft
detents around each completed mark, still position-driven. And reduced motion should give
the same six states as static or stepped panels, not simply disable everything.

## WHY IT FLOPPED, STRUCTURALLY

1. **"Settled" was declared before the eye voted.** The sheet says outright that nothing
   in it has been seen, then specifies gradients, keylight direction, slot depth, deckle,
   off-axis rotation, meniscus strength and fold physics. Those are **visual hypotheses**;
   writing them precisely does not convert them into decisions. It should have been titled
   *candidate physical model for still-frame test*, which makes revision expected rather
   than another failed settlement.
2. **The 14-agent fleet produced argumentative density, not quality.** "Beaten",
   "convicted", "discipline against", "the one failure this design cannot absorb", long
   prohibition lists — language that creates confidence on paper while making the design
   **reactive**. It optimises against every prior criticism until it is carrying defensive
   machinery. Coherence needs **fewer surviving intentions, not more defeated
   alternatives.**
3. **Product, interaction and art direction were settled simultaneously.** When the still
   frame fails, all of them destabilise together. That is the flop cycle. They need
   separate gates.

---

## ★ THE DIRECTION TO LOCK — the recessed showcase around the REAL card

Keep the sheet's strongest argument — derived input becoming an interpreted record — and
perform it **inside the already-approved spatial system**.

**1 · AT DEPTH.** Behind flush glass, the real card sits farther inside the Blue Room
surface. Mostly quiet: name, date, six empty positions. The surrounding room stays the
dominant architecture — which proves the showcase belongs to the main menu rather than
replacing it.

**2 · DERIVATION.** The six marks resolve one at a time. A narrow rail, engraved edge or
glass margin carries the compact working. One mark resolves, its interpretation follows,
then both rest. **The existing card accumulates the results.** No separate letter, plate
or detached tiles are necessary.

**3 · PRESENTATION.** Once complete, the card advances slightly toward the glass, or the
light changes enough to make it available. Its existing effects and controls become
active. The visitor opens the real reading.

> Nothing transforms into a substitute record. **The showcased object becomes the usable
> object.**

**Keeps:** derivation · reversibility · depth · the still hold · runtime integrity · a
visible final record.
**Removes:** the second product language · questionable fold physics · letter ambiguity ·
wet-metal behaviour · the need to reconstruct the existing card.

---

## HOW THE SHEET SHOULD BE REWRITTEN

Only these sections, in this order:

1. **Job** — one sentence on what the visitor should understand.
2. **Locked context** — main menu unchanged; showcase added; real card preserved;
   recessed glass model retained.
3. **Hero** — name the ONE object carrying the experience.
4. **Physical law** — surface, depth, light, motion. **One law, not several metaphors.**
5. **Three states** — at depth → deriving → available.
6. **Truth contract** — all displayed values from the reading engine; fictional fixture
   clearly identified; no typed examples in production.
7. **Interaction contract** — position-based, reversible, readable plateaus,
   keyboard/touch/reduced-motion behaviour.
8. **Handoff** — precisely what happens when the final card becomes actionable.
9. **Kill criteria** — reject if the still frame weakens the main menu, disguises the real
   card, or needs explanation to understand front/back/depth.

Rejected alternatives and repo findings move to appendices. Useful records; they must not
dominate the concept's positive shape.

# ★ THE MAIN LOOP'S NOTE — the convergence worth naming

The direction this audit locks is **the depth model already built and sitting in this
repo**. `transfer/COLOGNE-ACCORD-V2.md` §5 specifies exactly three planes — the original
menu surface, the object recessed *behind* it, and protective glass *flush with the
original surface* — with the recess made by inner occlusion and never a decorative frame.
Its §5 also records that **preservation of the real main menu scored 9.8/10** and is "the
constraint most easily lost in implementation," which is precisely the constraint the
concept sheet lost.

So the audit is not asking for a new system. It is asking U1 to use the Accord's system,
with the **real card** as the object at depth instead of a bottle. `_m2-accord.js` /
`_m2-accord.css` are a working implementation of that three-plane recess today.

**Consequence for sequencing:** the still frame this audit asks for is cheaper than it
looks, because the container already exists and is being refined. Finish the recess on the
Accord, then put the card in it.
