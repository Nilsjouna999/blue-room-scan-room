# THE MASTER, REVISED — THE INHABITED ORIGINAL v2

Supersedes `_m1-master2-sheet.md`. Written after the 27-agent audit; every change below is either an audit finding that survived the refuter, a builder's-signal defect, or an explicit overrule with its reason. The refuter's struck findings (COLOUR's "invented gold," ARIA's label-linting, the 44px complaints on decorative marks, the S4/S7/S15 "case" flag, the 4K-island taste call, the JS-off exits scenario, the speculative `barGlyph` typo-of-the-future in its original framing) are ignored as instructed, and I say so here once.

### NAME
**THE INHABITED ORIGINAL v2** — the shipped desk repaired in place, the archive alive inside the two sections **on the wide grid only**, the goods quoted verbatim, and the shipped stage machinery — the live pull, the drifting questions, the face turn — **kept, not replaced**.

### THESIS
The builder's resolution, taken literally and now also taken *whole*: the original's structure is the premium — so the master stops replacing the parts of the original that already work. The shipped stage (live deck, real dealt card, the free demo) is the strongest conversion asset on the page and v1 quietly deleted it; v2 keeps it as the single owner of the berth. What v2 adds is exactly what the shipped page lacks: two dense, adjacent, comparable door-sections that quote real engine output; one typographic exits row in place of the three pills; the critique's twelve repairs; and a phone layout that is *designed*, not the desktop stack squeezed. The field mechanic survives only where it was ever measured to work — the wide grid — with every mark's position **authored into data**, so nothing is drawn at load and the copy's "nothing is re-rolled" becomes true instead of ironic.

### GATE DESCRIPTION (no banned words)
A dark, silent ground. One lit parchment object stands in the middle distance, filled edge to edge with what it says — never a blank sheet — and it wears its name beneath it. Near it, close enough to read against each other in one glance at every size of screen, the two ways in: each carries its name, one true sentence, a quoted line of what it actually says, and — on a wide screen — a small settled shoal of the real glyphs it is read from, each glyph in its own permanent place, stirring toward gold when that way in is looked at. Under the object, five lines of reading, written by the same hand that turns the card. Beneath everything, one quiet line of words that are doors. Nothing is boxed that is not an object; nothing arrives; nothing performs; the page is set and clickable in the first painted frame.

### THE ARRANGEMENT

**Cascade direction: the shipped one.** v1's mobile-first inversion forced a ~720-line rewrite of `styles.css:3198–3921` and produced the broken stacked base every phone got. v2 keeps the live desktop-first cascade (wide rules unconditional, `@media (max-width:1199px)` stacks down at the existing seam) and edits the two regions that change. This is the critique's own verdict — *repair, don't rebuild* — applied to the CSS as well as the design.

**Wide (≥1200px), the inhabited layout:**
- `grid-template-columns: minmax(300px, 1fr) auto 330px`, areas
  `"mast stage head" / "tarot stage head" / "birth stage exits"` — the critique's item-7 track swap, unchanged from v1 (the one thing every judge agreed worked).
- **DOM source order: mast → tarot → birth → stage → head(berth) → exits.** The named areas place them; the source order makes the two readings adjacent in the accessible tree and in Tab order at every width, and kills the ≥1200px focus zigzag (WCAG 2.4.3 finding). This order is the stacked order too — one order, both layouts.
- `.m1exits` gets `align-self:end` inside its area so the exits sit at the foot of the third column rather than floating over ~320px of dead strip.
- H1 gets `text-wrap: balance`.

**The section fields — wide grid only, frozen into data:**
- Tarot section 16 marks (4 suits × 3 sizes from the codex.html `<defs>`, verbatim + 4 `#bd-major`), birth section 12 (two of each of six systems, real `data-sig` bar glyphs for trigram/hexagram). Sizes 11–18px, opacity .17–.36, stroke `#9c9790`.
- **Positions are authored literals.** Each mark's row is `[cid, glyph, sizeIndex, left%, top%, opacity]`. The PRNG (`mulberry32`) is deleted entirely. Positions are diffable in git, permanent across edits, and independent per mark — adding a 17th tarot mark moves nothing. This is the Spatial Law's fix and it makes S19's "Nothing is re-rolled on re-view" a true sentence.
- **The parting is enforced at every width the field exists at.** The 24px `rectDist` eviction runs at build *and* on the (debounced, existing) resize path, against live text rects, whenever the fields are rendered — the `innerWidth <= 430` gate is gone. Positions are *authored to pass* at the wide grid's minimum section width, so eviction is a guard, not the mechanism; any violating mark is hidden and reported by `console.error` (the team-5 audit pattern, now covering geometry as well as ids).
- `@media (forced-colors: active){ .m1sect__field{ display:none } }` — the shoal's entire recession system is opacity and hue, which forced-colors deletes; without this rule Windows High Contrast paints 28 full-contrast glyphs across the reading band.
- **Below 1200px there are no shoals.** Not thinned — absent, by design (see AT 430px). The base layout is the offer; the wide grid is the offer inhabited. This ends the marks-on-text failure at 431–1199px and the delete-all-28 failure below 431px in one move.

**The stir (the kept mechanic):** hovering, focusing, or first-touching a door lifts that section's marks to `--gold` over 220ms **and calls `m2SetFace(host, face, {})` — the shipped function.** v1's separate `address()` writer is demoted to a thin dispatcher that stirs the marks and calls `m2SetFace`; it never writes `berthMeta`/`berthMean` itself. **One owner of the berth and the face: the shipped path.** This resolves the two-writers conflict (SHIP §5) and keeps critique items 2 and 6 where they already live. Mouse 90ms debounce; focus immediate with the live region muted 120ms; touch `passive:true`, never `preventDefault` on a door.

**Marks are doors, lightly:** each mark keeps its real `data-cid` → `codex.html#cid` (the target ids are verified — this was the file's strongest law and it stays), `tabindex="-1"`, two-step naming on touch-primary into the fixed 16px foot slot. The fields sit **last** in each section's DOM (they are absolutely positioned; the move is free) so the door is announced before its decoration, and each field keeps its `role="img"` + true aria-label.

**Wiring is re-entrant.** All listeners are delegated from the panel root and bound remove-then-add inside the same mount path that already does this for `onMenuAnnexClick` (`app.js:3267` pattern), because `mountMenu()` re-renders on card-file and holdings change (`app.js:4882/4925`). The field build is wrapped: an unmapped cid or missing bits skips that one mark with a `console.error`, never aborts the script.

**State is read, not assumed.** At render, the template reads the same flags the rooms read: `m2SittingUsed()` / `br_dr_sitting_used`. If the free sitting is spent, S6 renders its spent variant (below) — the front door must not tell a returning customer their first is free when the room one click away knows it isn't.

Comparability is structural: both sections share one skeleton — kicker · name (h3) · sentence · specimen · what-it's-read-from line · way — with exactly one declared extra each (tarot: the Deep Read block; birth: the six links *are* its what-it's-read-from line).

### AT 430px — AND EVERYWHERE BELOW 1200px

- Touch targets: 44px on doors, deep link, exits, flip where present; the six birth links 24px inline floor. Exits wrap with flex `gap`, no negative margins — the stray wrapped `·` dies with them.
